Research Areas

What we study

Open Research Lab

Focus

MadGAA-LAB studies how AI agents behave in high-stakes, human-facing settings — starting with healthcare — and how to measure that behavior reliably.

We publish our tools, scenarios, and results openly so that others can reproduce, challenge, and build on them.

Research Areas

Multi-Agent Clinical Simulation

Simulated patients, clinicians, and examiners that interact through dialogue, used to train and test medical AI agents before real-world use.

  • patient persona design and adversarial scenarios
  • OSCE-style structured clinical examinations
  • real-time voice interaction

Related projects: OSCE-Project, OSCE Real-Time Voice

Evaluation & Benchmarks

Methods for scoring agent behavior on empathy, persuasion, safety, and task success, with public leaderboards for fair comparison.

  • LLM-as-judge rubrics and their reliability
  • standardized scenario sets
  • open leaderboards

Related projects: OSCE-AgentBeats Leaderboard

Healthcare Data Agents

Agents that retrieve and reason over structured clinical records using interoperability standards.

  • FHIR database querying
  • retrieval-augmented clinical reasoning

Related projects: FHIR-Agent

Agent Safety & Red-Teaming

Testing how agents hold up against prompt injection, manipulation, and other adversarial pressure.

  • attack and defense scenarios
  • robustness evaluation

Related projects: AgentBeats Security Arena

Efficient Models

Making capable models small and fast enough to run on local and edge hardware.

  • knowledge distillation and domain fine-tuning
  • hardware-aware optimization and inference benchmarking

Related projects: Model Training & Distillation
